Wired Village/About

About Wired Village

Wired Village is a community for people who want to learn hardware, IoT and OT/ICS security, supported by Traboda CyberLabs.

What we do

We run hands-on labs, workshops and hardware CTFs at security events. Participants work directly on real devices, intentionally vulnerable hardware and portable OT ranges, covering embedded and firmware security, wireless, industrial protocols and critical infrastructure.

Who builds the labs

The labs are built and run by a community of volunteers. Members of the bi0s hardware team at Amrita, alumni and contributors from across the security community come together and collaborate to make each lab happen.

The badges

Each badge targets a different corner of hardware security, and every challenge is documented on this site with the exact header, pin and tool it expects.

BadgePlatformCovers
Compatriot ESP32-C6 + STM32F103 CAN/UDS over Ethernet, UART, SPI, I²C, SWD, BLE and WiFi
Sysrupt V2 ESP32-S3 “Vanguard” OT/ICS: Modbus, OPC-UA, GOOSE and SNMP
Sysrupt V1 Portable OT range ICS/SCADA: Modbus, DNP3, S7comm and IEC 104

The first few challenges on every page are fully open, walkthrough included, so you can see how the guides work and get moving. From there on you get the objective and a hint, and the full walkthrough stays locked behind the passphrase handed out at the event. Board diagrams are generated from the KiCad project, so every ring, pin number and pin 1 marker sits where it does on the board in your hands.
